# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-27

### Features
- First version of the TV guide card (`custom:tv-guide-card`) and its mini card (`custom:tv-guide-mini-card`): tonight's programme (or what is on now) on each channel, with the genre and the times, a progress bar while on air and the programme that follows.
- EPG sources: XMLTV EPG, Programme TNT FR, EPG (open-epg.com), calendars, and template or RESTful sensors, mixed as needed; the channel's name and logo come from the integration when it gives them.
- Genre filters (movie, series, sport, kids, news, documentary, show), from the guide's category or found in the programme's text in the card's 22 languages.
- Optional play button: the channel's stream on a media player, a source, a script or any action; the channel being watched is highlighted.
- Illustrated header: a living room whose television shows a changing picture on air, the evening's first time before it, a dark screen with nothing on; light and dark themes.
- Visual editor for both cards; 22 languages.
